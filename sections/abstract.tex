\begin{abstract}
\normalsize
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{1em}
We study optimal decision trees and partial set covering under precedence constraints. By this we mean that selecting a test/set requires selecting all its predecessors first. We consider both worst-case and uniform average-case identification cost for decision trees, as well as cover size and total coverage time for covering problems.

To design approximation algorithms for these tasks we first relate decision tree construction to set covering: we show that worst-case and average-case identification reduce to cover size and total coverage time, respectively. We then prove that the latter two objectives can be further reduced to the well-known \textup{\textsc{Partial Red-Blue Set Cover}} problem. We give an improved bicriteria algorithm for this task that yields $\bigo\br{m^{1/3}\cdot\log n}$-approximation algorithms for all four objectives.
% For both covering objectives, we also obtain bicriteria $\br{\bigo\br{m^{1/3}},8}$-approximations, covering at least one eighth of the required number of elements. 
Here $m$ is the number of tests/sets, and $n$ is the number of hypotheses/universe elements. We also obtain polylogarithmic approximation guarantees for inforest and outforest precedence constraints.

To complement these results, we show that under the \textsc{Planted Dense Subgraph Conjecture}, no polynomial-time algorithm achieves approximation factors of $\bigo(n^{1/2-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/4-\epsilon})$ for any $\epsilon>0$ and all four objectives. For \textsc{Precedence-Constrained Min-Sum Set Cover}, these strengthen the $\bigo(n^{1/6-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/12-\epsilon})$ bounds of McClintock, Mestre and Wirth [ISAAC 2017]. For deterministic algorithms, a new reduction from \textup{\textsc{Label Cover}} excludes polylogarithmic approximation factors unless $\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}$, factors $\bigo(2^{(\log x)^{1-\epsilon}})$ assuming $\mathrm{NP}\not\subseteq \mathrm{DTIME}\left(2^{(\log L)^{O(1)}}\right)$, for $x\in\{n,m\}$ and $0<\epsilon<1$, and factors $\bigo(x^\delta)$ for some constant $\delta>0$ under the Projection Games Conjecture and $\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}$. Our improved lower bounds hold for both covering objectives, every fixed positive target fraction, even for bicriteria guarantees, and transfer to both decision tree cost objectives. We also establish conditional, polylogarithmic hardness bounds for outforest precedence constraints. 
\end{abstract}
