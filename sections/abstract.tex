\begin{abstract}
This work considers a number of optimization problems and reductive relations between them.
The two main problems we are interested in are the \textsc{Optimal Decision Tree} and \textsc{Set Cover}.
We study these two fundamental tasks under precedence constraints, that is, if a test (or set) $X$ is a predecessor of $Y$, then in any feasible decision tree $X$ needs to be an ancestor of $Y$  (or respectively, if $Y$ is added to set cover, then so must be $X$).
For the \textsc{Optimal Decision Tree} we consider two optimization criteria: worst case identification time (height of the tree) or the average identification time.
Similarly, for the \textsc{Set Cover} we study two cost measures: the size of the cover or the average cover time.

Our approach is to develop a number of algorithmic reductions, where an approximation algorithm for one problem provides an approximation for another via a black-box usage of a procedure for the former.
En route we introduce other optimization problems either to complete the `reduction landscape' or because they hold the essence of combinatorial structure of our problems.
At the end of this algorithmic scheme we reduce the problem to the 
 \textsc{Partial Red-Blue Set Cover} and show that we can leverage the result of [APPROX 2023] to obtain $\mathcal{O}^*(m^{1/3})$-approximation polynomial-time algorithms for all aforementioned problems.
Conversely, we also obtain inapproximability results for all of the aforementioned problem. We generalize and refine the known constuction of [ISAAC 2017], to improve the inapproximability bounds from $\mathcal{O}(n^{1/6-\epsilon})$- and $\mathcal{O}(m^{1/12-\epsilon})$ to $\mathcal{O}(n^{1/2-\epsilon})$- and $\mathcal{O}(m^{1/4-\epsilon})$, for all our objectives, conditioned on the Planted Dense Subgraph Conjecture. We also give an entirely new reduction from \textsc{Label Cover}, which excludes polylogarithmic approximations under $\text{P}\ne\text{NP}$, $\mathcal{O}\left(2^{(\log n)^{1-\epsilon}}\right)$- and $\mathcal{O}\left(2^{(\log m)^{1-\epsilon}}\right)$-approximations under ETH for $0<\epsilon<1$, and polynomial hardness under the quantitatively stronger \textsc{Projection Games Conjecture}. For inforests and outforests, we give polylogarithmic approximation guarantees with corresponding hardness results for all relevant variants.
\end{abstract}
