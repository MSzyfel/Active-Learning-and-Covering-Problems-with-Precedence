\begin{abstract}
\normalsize
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{1em}
We study optimal decision trees and partial set covering under precedence constraints. By this we mean that a test may be performed only after its predecessors, and similarily, selecting a set requires selecting its predecessors. We consider worst-case and uniform average-case identification cost for decision trees, as well as cover size and total coverage time for covering problems.

In this work we paint an algorithmic landscape of the approximability of these tasks. En route we show how these objective relate to each other, i.e., both worst-case and average-case identification objectives reduce to cover size and total coverage time, respectively. Then, we show that both of these can be further reduced to a well-known \textsc{Partial Red-Blue Set Cover} problem. We give a deterministic bicriteria algorithm for this problem that yields $\bigo\br{m^{1/3}\cdot\log n}$-approximation algorithms for all four objectives. For both covering objectives, we also obtain bicriteria $\br{\bigo\br{m^{1/3}},8}$-approximations, covering at least one eighth of the required number of elements. Here $m$ is the number of tests or sets, and $n$ is the number of hypotheses or universe elements. We also obtain polylogarithmic approximation guarantees for inforest and outforest precedence constraints.

To complement these result, we show that Under the Planted Dense Subgraph Conjecture, no polynomial-time algorithm achieves approximation factors $\bigo(n^{1/2-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/4-\epsilon})$ for every $\epsilon>0$ and all four objectives. For precedence-constrained min-sum set cover, these strengthen the $\bigo(n^{1/6-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/12-\epsilon})$ bounds of McClintock, Mestre and Wirth [ISAAC 2017]. These lower bounds hold for every fixed positive target fraction, even for bicriteria guarantees. For deterministic algorithms, a new reduction from \textsc{Label Cover} excludes polylogarithmic approximation factors unless $\text{P}=\text{NP}$, factors $\bigo(2^{(\log x)^{1-\epsilon}})$ assuming $\text{NP}\subsetneq DTIME\left(2^{(\log L)^{O(1)}}\right)$, for $x\in\{n,m\}$ and $0<\epsilon<1$, and factors $\bigo(x^\delta)$ for some constant $\delta>0$ under the Projection Games Conjecture. We also establish conditional hardness bounds for outforest precedence constraints.
\end{abstract}
