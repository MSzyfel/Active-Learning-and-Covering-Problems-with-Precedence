\begin{abstract}
\normalsize
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{1em}
We study optimal decision trees and partial set covering under precedence constraints. A test may be performed only after its predecessors, and selecting a set requires selecting its predecessors. We consider worst-case and uniform average-case identification cost for decision trees, and cover size and total coverage time for covering problems.

We relate these four objectives through algorithmic reductions to \textsc{Partial Red-Blue Set Cover}. Applying the approximation algorithm of Chlamt{\'{a}}{\v{c}}, Makarychev and Vakilian [APPROX 2023] gives randomized $\widetilde{\bigo}(m^{1/3})$-approximation algorithms whose guarantees hold with high probability for all four objectives. Here $m$ is the number of tests or sets, $n$ is the number of hypotheses or universe elements, and $\widetilde{\bigo}$ suppresses factors polylogarithmic in $m$ and $n$. We also obtain polylogarithmic approximation guarantees for inforest and outforest precedence constraints.

Under the Planted Dense Subgraph Conjecture, no polynomial-time algorithm achieves approximation factors $\bigo(n^{1/2-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/4-\epsilon})$ for every $\epsilon>0$ and all four objectives. For precedence-constrained min-sum set cover, these strengthen the $\bigo(n^{1/6-\epsilon})$ and $\bigo(m^{1/12-\epsilon})$ bounds of McClintock, Mestre and Wirth [ISAAC 2017]. The covering bounds hold for every fixed positive target fraction, even with any fixed loss in coverage. For deterministic algorithms, a new reduction from \textsc{Label Cover} excludes polylogarithmic approximation factors unless $\text{P}=\text{NP}$, factors $\bigo(2^{(\log x)^{1-\epsilon}})$ assuming $\text{NP}$ is not contained in deterministic quasipolynomial time, for $x\in\{n,m\}$ and $0<\epsilon<1$, and factors $\bigo(x^\delta)$ for some constant $\delta>0$ under the Projection Games Conjecture and $\text{P}\ne\text{NP}$. We also establish conditional hardness bounds for outforest precedence constraints.
\end{abstract}
