\paragraph*{AI methodology.}%\label{sec:ai-methodology.}
We used OpenAI Codex to assist with exposition, notation, and manuscript review, and with developing, simplifying, and checking portions of the arguments in Sections~\ref{sec:AL}-\ref{sec:conclusions}. This assistance included reduction proofs, probability estimates, and parameter calculations. The authors read, understood and checked all AI-generated writeup and reasoning.
